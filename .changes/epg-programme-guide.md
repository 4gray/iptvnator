The programme guide now shows your playlist's own channels in their order,
with the current group or favorites one click away. Click a channel to
switch playback while the player stays on screen above the grid;
double-click switches and closes. Open it from the new Guide button, the
header, the command palette, or the G key. Hide channels without EPG and
pick comfortable or compact rows.

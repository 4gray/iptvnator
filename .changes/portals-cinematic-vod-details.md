Movie and series pages open on a cinematic backdrop with chips, plot and one action row: a light Continue/Play button naming the next episode and the time left, a Trailer modal, and a "…" menu with other sources, external player, copy URL, reset progress and season actions. Cast & crew and Similar follow as rows. Without a 16:9 backdrop the hero shrinks to its content over the blurred poster.

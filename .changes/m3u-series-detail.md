highlight: Watch M3U series by season and episode
---

The Series section of an M3U playlist now shows shows rather than episode rows: a playlist whose provider ships 40,000 episode lines becomes a catalog of around 2,000 series. Opening one gives the same season tabs, episode grid and inline player the Xtream and Stalker series pages use, and playback runs through the existing path with the playlist's own headers.

The app now remembers your zoom level (Cmd/Ctrl and +/-, Cmd/Ctrl+0 to
reset): it holds when you switch between sections or resize the window, and
comes back after a restart, so a larger font size no longer resets to the
default. Windows and Linux gain the zoom shortcuts, which had only worked
through the macOS menu.

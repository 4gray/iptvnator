highlight: Channel list in fullscreen

Rest the mouse on the left edge (tap it on touch) or press C while a live
channel plays fullscreen: the channel list (M3U all/groups/favorites/recent,
Xtream, Stalker, global favorites) slides over the video with search, so you
can zap without leaving fullscreen. Nothing covers the video while it is
closed. PageUp/PageDown also switch channels.

highlight: Channel list in fullscreen

Rest the mouse on the left edge, click the edge handle, or press C while a
live channel plays fullscreen: the channel list (M3U with all/groups/favorites/
recent, Xtream, Stalker and global favorites) slides over the video with
search, so you can zap without leaving fullscreen. PageUp/PageDown also switch
channels, and switching no longer drops out of fullscreen.

Continue Watching no longer lists movies you have finished. A series moves on
to the episode after the one you watched last, ignoring extras (season 0), and
leaves after its final episode. A title counts as finished once 90% of it has
played, as in Plex, Jellyfin and Kodi, so the end credits can be skipped. A
series page's play button picks the same episode.

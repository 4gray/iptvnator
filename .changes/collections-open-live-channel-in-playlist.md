A live channel you watch from Favorites or Recently viewed now says which playlist it belongs to: a chip with the playlist name sits in the programme panel next to the channel, and clicking it (or "Open in <playlist>" in the channel's right-click menu) jumps to that channel inside its own playlist. Works for Xtream and M3U sources; Stalker follows in a later release.

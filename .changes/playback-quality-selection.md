The built-in web players' shared controls gain a quality menu: multi-bitrate
HLS and DASH streams can be pinned to a specific rendition ("1080p", "720p", …)
or left on Auto, which keeps the player's adaptive selection. The menu appears
only when the stream actually offers more than one quality, and the choice
lasts for the current playback session.

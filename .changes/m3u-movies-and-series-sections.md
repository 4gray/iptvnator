highlight: Movies and Series for M3U playlists
---

M3U playlists carrying films or episodes now get their own Movies and Series sections beside All channels and Groups, each with a group rail, a poster grid and section-wide search. Films can be favourited from their detail view, and locked groups stay behind the parental PIN. Live-only playlists are unchanged; the sections can be turned off in Settings → General.

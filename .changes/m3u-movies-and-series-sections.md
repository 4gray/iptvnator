highlight: Movies and Series for M3U playlists
---

M3U playlists carrying films or episodes now get their own Movies and Series sections beside All channels and Groups, so the live list stops being a scroll through everything the provider ships. Each has its own group rail and poster grid, and search spans the whole section. Live-only playlists are unchanged; the sections can be turned off in Settings → General.

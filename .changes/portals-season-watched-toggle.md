Series detail pages can now mark a whole season as watched (or unwatched) in
one click — the new button next to "Download season" works for both Xtream and
Stalker portals. Handy when you start a show mid-way: mark the earlier seasons
watched and the Play button jumps straight to your next unwatched episode.

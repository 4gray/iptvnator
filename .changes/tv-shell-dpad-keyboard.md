On Android TV and Fire TV, moving the remote's focus onto a text field no longer pops up the on-screen keyboard and traps the next arrow press. Press OK on the field to open the keyboard.

In fullscreen, the remote now reaches the player controls (D-pad and OK did nothing before), and the blue focus ring no longer frames the whole screen. Moving between the Live TV rail, categories, channels and the preview picture lands on the selected or first entry instead of whichever one is level with the focus, and Left on a live picture returns to the playing channel.

Unsaved settings edits no longer vanish silently when you close the window,
quit the app, or reload the page. The desktop app now asks whether to save,
discard, or keep editing — the same dialog in-app navigation shows — and only
closes once your choice is applied; a failed save keeps the window open. In the
browser, the native leave-page warning appears instead.

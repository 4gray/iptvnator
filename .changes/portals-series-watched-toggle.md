The series pages' season header now has a ⋮ menu that marks the WHOLE series
as watched (or unwatched) in one click, for both Xtream and Stalker portals.
On Ministra portals that load seasons lazily, the app first fetches the
remaining seasons and then marks everything — so catching up on a show you
started mid-way is a single action.

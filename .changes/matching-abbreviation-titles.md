Movies whose name is a short abbreviation — AKA, RRR, IO, VFW, Y2K — are no
longer mistaken for a language tag and filed under their release year alone.
They now match their own copies in your other playlists, so alternative
sources, recommendations and pinned sources land on the right film instead of
an unrelated one that happens to share that year.

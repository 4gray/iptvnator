Desktop Xtream sources let you choose which Movies, Live TV and Series catalogs to load, including none. Newly enabled types refresh on save with scanning progress; disabled types stay out of browsing and search while saved Favorites, history and downloads are kept. After scanning, the first newly enabled type opens in sidebar order; reopening a source also respects its selection.

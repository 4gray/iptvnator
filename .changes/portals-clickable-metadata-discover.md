Year, genre, and country on movie and series detail pages are now clickable
(with TMDB metadata enabled): each chip opens a Discover page of popular
titles for that facet, marked with what's already in your library — matches
jump straight to their detail page, the rest open the portal search. Works
in Xtream and Stalker, like the existing actor and director chips.

Settings are easier to scan: the sidebar groups pages into App, Library,
Devices and Data with About pinned at the bottom, each page sorts its rows into
titled cards with one aligned control column, and checkboxes became switches.
Reset now lives on "Backup & data", subtitles moved to Playback, and the save
bar counts your unsaved changes and answers Cmd/Ctrl+S. Esc leaves settings.

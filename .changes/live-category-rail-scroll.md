Clicking a visible Live TV category (such as a provider-pinned
"UK|Movies" at the top of the rail) no longer scrolls the category rail
away — the rail only moves when the selected category is actually out of
view, and it follows the newly rendered selection instead of the
previous one. The same applies to the M3U groups rail and the fullscreen
episode list: a visible selected row no longer pulls the list away.
